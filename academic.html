Academic Program

Explore the Academic Program and learn more about its areas of learning, academic preparation, research, communication, and possible pathways for further education.

About the Academic Program

The Academic Program is intended for learners who want to strengthen their academic knowledge, communication skills, research abilities, and preparation for further education.

What is the Academic Program?

The Academic Program provides learners with opportunities to develop knowledge and skills through academic learning, research, communication, analysis, and other learning activities.

It can help learners prepare for their future educational goals while developing skills that may be useful in college and other forms of higher education.

What Can Learners Develop?

📖 Academic Knowledge

Build knowledge and understanding through classroom learning and academic activities.

🔬 Research Skills

Develop skills in gathering information, analyzing ideas, preparing research outputs, and presenting findings.

🗣️ Communication

Strengthen written and oral communication skills through presentations, discussions, and academic activities.

🎓 College Preparation

Develop academic habits and skills that may help learners prepare for further education.

Academic Areas

The current ACSHS website identifies the following Academic-related areas.

STEM Related

An area for learners interested in science, technology, mathematics, problem-solving, investigation, and related academic activities.

HUMSS Related

An area for learners interested in humanities, social sciences, communication, society, culture, and related academic activities.

ABM Related

An area for learners interested in business, accounting, management, entrepreneurship, organization, and related academic activities.

Hands-on Expertise

An area identified on the current website for learning activities that emphasize practical application and development of skills.

Skills for Future Learning

  • Critical thinking
  • Research and information gathering
  • Written and oral communication
  • Problem-solving
  • Collaboration
  • Academic presentation
