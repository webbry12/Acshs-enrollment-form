TechPro Track

Explore practical, technical, and hands-on learning opportunities available through the TechPro program.

About TechPro

TechPro focuses on practical and technical learning through hands-on activities, projects, application of skills, and technology-related learning.

What is TechPro?

The TechPro program provides learners with opportunities to develop practical and technical skills through activities that emphasize application and practice.

What Can You Learn?

💻 Computer Skills

Develop computer-related knowledge, technical skills, troubleshooting, and technology-based activities.

🔧 Practical Skills

Develop skills through hands-on activities, projects, tools, materials, and practical exercises.

🛠️ Technical Skills

Learn technical procedures and apply knowledge to practical situations.

💼 Career Skills

Build practical skills that can support future training, work, or further education.

TechPro Specializations

💻 ICT / Computer Systems Servicing

Focuses on computer-related activities, systems, troubleshooting, and technical skills.

🏠 Home Economics

Focuses on practical activities, services, livelihood, and home-economics-related skills.

🔨 Industrial Arts

Focuses on hands-on activities involving tools, materials, technical processes, and projects.

⚙️ Other TechPro Specialization

Other technical and practical learning areas may be available depending on the school's offerings.

Skills You Can Develop

  • Technical problem-solving
  • Practical application
  • Computer and technology skills
  • Project development
  • Hands-on skills
  • Collaboration
  • Technical communication
