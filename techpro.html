TechPro Program

Explore practical, technical, and career-oriented learning opportunities through hands-on activities and skills development.

About the TechPro Program

The TechPro Program focuses on practical, technical, and hands-on learning. It is intended for learners who want to develop useful skills through application, practice, and technical activities.

What is TechPro?

TechPro provides opportunities for learners interested in developing practical and technical skills.

Learning activities may involve hands-on practice, technical procedures, problem-solving, and application of skills in different situations.

What Can Learners Develop?

🛠️ Practical Skills

Develop skills through activities that involve practice and application.

💻 Technical Skills

Learn technical concepts, procedures, tools, and technology-related activities.

🔧 Hands-on Learning

Apply concepts through practical activities, demonstrations, projects, and exercises.

💼 Career Preparation

Build practical skills that may support future career, training, or further educational goals.

TechPro Specializations

The current ACSHS website identifies the following TechPro-related areas.

💻 ICT / Computer Systems Servicing

An area focused on computer and information-technology-related practical skills, including computer systems, troubleshooting, and related technical activities.

🏠 Home Economics

An area focused on practical skills and activities related to home economics, services, livelihood, and related applications.

🔨 Industrial Arts

An area focused on practical and technical activities involving tools, materials, processes, construction-related skills, and hands-on projects.

⚙️ Other TechPro Specialization

Other technical and practical specializations may be presented according to the school's available program offerings.

Skills You Can Develop

  • Technical problem-solving
  • Practical application
  • Equipment and tool awareness
  • Project development
  • Collaboration
  • Workplace-oriented skills
  • Technical communication
