About the School

Learn more about Antipolo City Senior High School and this online enrollment portal.

Antipolo City Senior High School

Antipolo City Senior High School (ACSHS) provides senior high school learners with opportunities to develop academic knowledge, practical skills, creativity, research abilities, and preparation for their future educational and career goals.

Through its learning programs and school activities, learners are encouraged to develop their skills, participate in meaningful learning experiences, and prepare for their next stage of education or career development.

This website serves as an online enrollment portal where prospective learners can explore the available programs and access the online enrollment form.

Academic & TechPro Programs

Explore the learning focus, strands, skills, and preparation offered through the two program pathways of ACSHS.

📚

Academic Program

The Academic Program is designed for learners who want to strengthen their academic knowledge, communication, research, and study skills while preparing for college and higher education.

What learners can expect

  • Academic-focused learning and study skills
  • Research, writing, and communication activities
  • Development of analytical and critical-thinking skills
  • Preparation for college and higher education
  • Learning experiences connected to different academic fields

Academic areas listed on this portal

STEM Related Science, technology, engineering, and mathematics-related learning and interests.
ASSH Related Arts, Social Sciences, and Humanities communication, society, culture, and people-centered learning.
BE Related Accountancy, business, management, entrepreneurship, and related academic interests.

TechPro Program

The TechPro Program is designed for learners who are interested in practical, technical, hands-on, and career-oriented learning. It emphasizes the development of useful technical skills through practical activities.

What learners can expect

  • Practical and technical skills development
  • Hands-on learning activities
  • Application of skills to practical tasks
  • Technical problem-solving and troubleshooting
  • Preparation for career-oriented learning

TechPro specializations listed on this portal

ICT / Computer Systems Servicing Practical learning related to information and communications technology and computer systems servicing.
Home Economics Practical learning connected with home economics and related hands-on and livelihood skills.
Industrial Arts Hands-on technical learning involving tools, materials, practical work, and industrial arts skills.
